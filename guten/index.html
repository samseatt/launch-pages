Guten

Knowledge rendered into useful form.

Books have long carried thought across distance and time. Digital systems let a work do something more: remain readable as a book while becoming navigable, connected, revisable, and alive to the communities forming around it.

Guten is being developed as a home for books, living publications, and the forms between them. This interim edition begins with two related works.
